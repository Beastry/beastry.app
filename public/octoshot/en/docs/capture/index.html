Capture modes

Area capture

Choose Capture Area from the menu or your area hotkey. Drag a rectangle, then use the mode bar:

Key Mode
S Screenshot
R Scroll stitch (scroll to collect frames)
V Video (region)
G GIF (region)
L LaTeX formula capture
T Figure / table to Clips

Window & fullscreen

Capture Window highlights selectable windows. Capture Fullscreen captures the main display.

Quick Access

After capture, a floating thumbnail offers copy, save, annotate, dismiss, and drag-out. Apply runs the top on-device suggestion when available.

New captures sit at the bottom/front of the stack. Click to spread the cards upward; click outside or use Collapse stack to fold them back. Each image retains its aspect ratio. Removing a card with × or dragging it out does not delete its saved file.

In Settings → General → Quick access → Toolbar style, choose Below image (the default capsule) or Ring (a semi-transparent circular overlay centered on the hovered card, capped at 170 points in diameter and shrunk to fit the visible bounds). Its six equal slots offer Copy, Save, Annotate, Pin, OCR, and Close, with hover highlighting, tooltips, and accessibility labels. The ring appears above the cards with a spring scale/fade, reserving no space, opening no gaps, and causing no panel growth. Only the capsule opens a gap on expanded hover, using space reserved in advance; collapsed hover moves no cards. Extra AI/Markdown/path actions are available in the image's context menu.

Annotation

Open Annotate from Quick Access for arrows, shapes, text, mosaic, undo, and export.

Scroll capture

In scroll mode, scroll the target content manually, or enable auto-scroll in Settings → Recording (requires Accessibility).
