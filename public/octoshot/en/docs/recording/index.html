Recording & GIF

Fullscreen recording

From the menu bar:

  • Record Fullscreen (Video) — H.264 MP4 via ScreenCaptureKit
  • Record Fullscreen (GIF) — timed frames exported as GIF

A floating Stop panel and menu-bar indicator appear while recording.

Region video / GIF

Start Capture Area, then press V (video) or G (GIF) on the mode bar.

Settings

Settings → Recording controls cursor visibility, GIF frame rate, max width, and scroll-capture options.

Permissions

Screen Recording permission is required for all capture and recording features.
