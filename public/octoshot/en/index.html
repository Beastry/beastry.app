Get started in minutes

  1. 1

    Install & grant access

    Download the release, open once, and allow Screen Recording (and Accessibility if you use auto-scroll).

  2. 2

    Set your shortcuts

    Optionally take over ⌘⇧3/4/5 or define custom keys in Settings → Shortcuts.

  3. 3

    Capture & automate

    Use the menu bar or `octoshot` CLI; enable embedded models in Settings → AI when you need vision or LaTeX.

Questions

Is Octoshot free?

Yes. Octoshot is MIT-licensed open source. Releases are published on GitHub.

Does it upload screenshots to the cloud?

No. Capture and optional on-device models run locally unless you explicitly configure an external endpoint.

Who is 一酱?

Octoshot’s original mascot—a deadpan red cartoon octopus inspired by our app icon. Personality: complains with words, delivers with all eight arms (“grumbles aloud, ships quietly”). Your menu-bar screenshot butler.

Where is the documentation?

Guides will mirror the in-app Help Markdown (EN + 中文). A docs section on this site will sync from the repo.
